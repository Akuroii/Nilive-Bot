RISKS / OPEN DECISIONS (left explicit, not guessed)
  1. CONTRACT CHANGE: an unresolved role grant (intent `started`/`present`)
     now blocks refund until Discord shows the role absent after the 10-min
     settle window. An operator note alone no longer authorizes it.
  2. Role observed PRESENT: stays blocked; code neither removes it nor
     records a holding. Operator policy needed: remove role then refund, or
     record the holding then mark delivered.
  3. "Absent after the settle window" narrows but cannot prove that an earlier
     request will never be applied (SQLite and Discord cannot be atomic).
  4. Legacy operations (provenance_tracked=0) with same-name unlabelled goods
     remain fail-closed; no override added.
  5. `never_started` stranded rows are parked as `uncertain`, not auto-refunded
     (a slow worker in another process could still write goods).
  6. Mixed-version deployment: an OLD worker can still write `delivering`-
     unaware paths; only the DB trigger protects refund flags. Deploy bot and
     dashboard together.
  7. init_db now fails fatally on a lock/other migration error (PR behavior);
     no retry added (no failing path demonstrated).
  8. Booster: cached member state can lag the API; _give_role is not under the
     slot lock. Not changed (out of scope).
  9. The stranded lease (30 min) and settle window (10 min) are constants, not
     validated against production latencies.
