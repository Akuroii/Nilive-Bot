Keep role vs conflicting roles

When a member ends up holding both sides of a rule, the keep role wins and only the conflicting roles listed here are removed. Rules are evaluated when a role is added — saving or editing a rule never touches members who already hold these roles.

A conflicting role is never removed while another Nilive system still grants it (Level reward, Prestige, Booster, Shop/timed ownership, an active Twitch session, the current MVP award, or a timed reaction role). Those removals are skipped and logged instead of silently revoking an earned role. Roles managed by a reaction-role panel cannot be used as conflicting roles at all, because an untimed reaction role keeps no ownership record to verify against.
